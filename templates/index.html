What you're seeing

  • Circulation. The Kutta condition makes flow leave the trailing edge smoothly, creating net circulation Γ; upper-surface flow is faster (warm colours = low pressure = suction).
  • Bernoulli vs Newton: lower pressure up top pulls the wing up; equivalently the streamlines are turned downward and lift is the reaction to that momentum change. Both give L = ρ V∞ Γ b.
  • Stall: past α ≈ 15° the boundary layer separates in real life, circulation collapses, lift falls — watch the chart fold over while the stats turn red.
